The marriage

2

The date of the marriage and the marriage settlement decide the property regime, and the regime decides what is even in the estate. Everything else on this sheet can be estimated and refined later. These two cannot be guessed — get them exact, and ask for the prenup itself rather than the client's memory of it.

Before 3 Aug 1988 → Conjugal Partnership. On or after → Absolute Community.
A prenup overrides the date.

Existing life insurance

5

One card per policy — add as many as the client holds. Two fields decide whether a policy is even part of the estate: an irrevocable beneficiary puts the proceeds outside it entirely, and a lapsed policy is not cover at all. If the client is not certain a policy is in force, mark it to be verified rather than guessing — then verify it with the insurer before the recommendation.

Liquidity

6
Of everything we just listed, how much could your family turn into cash within twelve months — without selling anything at a loss?
Ask it out loud, in these words. Estate tax is due one year from death. This number is the difference between a plan and a forced sale.
