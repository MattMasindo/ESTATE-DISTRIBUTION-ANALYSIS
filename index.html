Where the coverage goes
    Absent a marriage settlement, the regime follows the date of marriage: before 3 August 1988 the Conjugal Partnership of Gains (Civil Code); on or after, the Absolute Community of Property (Family Code, Arts. 75 and 256). A valid pre-nuptial agreement overrides that default. The spouse's exclusive property never enters the estate, and her half of the shared property is hers outright — not inheritance. Collateral relatives are not modelled; they inherit only when no descendants, ascendants, spouse or illegitimate children survive. Estate tax is not yet applied. This is a planning aid, not legal advice.
